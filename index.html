Sign a word. SignSpeak says it out loud — in person or on a call.

Train it on a handful of signs, then watch it recognize them live through your webcam and speak them aloud. Works face-to-face, and in a built-in two-way video call with live captions.

Remote too

this barrier doesn't disappear on phone or video calls — a deaf person often can't communicate remotely without a third-party interpreter

Why this gap still exists

  • Human interpreters can't scale to every daily interaction
  • Learning sign language takes years — not realistic to ask of every shopkeeper, doctor, or stranger
  • Most sign-recognition research stays in academic papers or needs expensive hardware (gloves, depth sensors)
  • No simple tool exists that works both face-to-face and remotely, using devices people already own
No special gloves. No expensive sensors.
Just a camera and a browser.
How it works
1

Teach it a sign

Pick a word, hold the sign in front of your camera, and capture a few samples. Takes seconds per word.

2

It recognizes & speaks

Sign it again — SignSpeak matches it against what you taught it and speaks the word out loud instantly.

3

Take it to a call

Start a video call and share the link over WhatsApp or SMS — signs become speech, and speech becomes live captions, both ways.

Institutions

No LLMs. No GPT. No Claude.
This is a computer vision + classic ML pipeline, not an LLM-based app. No text generation, no chatbot, no external AI APIs.

Impact & future scope

  • Expandable vocabulary — the same pipeline scales to hundreds of signs with more training data
  • Multi-language speech output for regional accessibility
  • Full two-way calling already part of the core vision, not a future add-on
  • Real deployment potential in hospitals, government offices, and schools as a low-cost accessibility tool

Why this stands out

Unlike many hackathon accessibility projects that stay theoretical, SignSpeak is fully interactive and immediately demoable — judges can watch a sign happen and hear it spoken back in real time, live on stage. It combines genuine social impact with a technically credible AI pipeline and a realistic remote-communication feature, built entirely on free, open tools.

09. AI Models & Technologies

A combination of proven AI, simple ML, and built-in browser APIs — no LLM, no server.

1

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker (Google)

Hand Tracking (Computer Vision AI)
  • Pretrained deep learning model (CNN-based) by Google
  • Detects 21 precise key points on each hand in real time
  • Gives exact hand landmark coordinates to understand the sign
Used via CDN (MediaPipe Tasks Vision)
2

k-Nearest Neighbors (k-NN)

Sign Classification (Our Part)
  • Our own ML model
  • Takes hand landmark points from MediaPipe
  • Uses k-NN (similarity matching) to find which trained sign it most closely matches
  • Trained live on our own captured data
Simple. Fast. No GPU required.
3

Speech Synthesis (Browser API)

Text to Speech
  • Browser's built-in Speech Synthesis API (Chrome/Edge/Safari)
  • Converts the recognized word into natural speech instantly
  • No separate model or API key required
Built-in. Free. Works offline.
4

Speech Recognition (Browser API)

Speech to Text (for Calls)
  • Browser's built-in Speech Recognition API (webkit)
  • Converts the hearing user's speech to live text captions during calls
  • Used only in the SignSpeak Connect feature
Built-in. Real-time. No extra cost.
